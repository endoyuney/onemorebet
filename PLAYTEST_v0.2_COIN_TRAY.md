# v0.2 Coin Tray Playtest

This iteration changes only the Coin layer. Scratch is intentionally left unchanged until the Coin Tray is approved.

## What changed

- Removed the old move-up table model from the prototype build.
- Added three persistent denominations on one table:
  - Copper $1 — high hit rate / low volatility
  - Silver $5 — medium volatility
  - Gold $25 — low hit rate / high volatility / stronger jackpot pressure
- Silver unlock target: peak $60.
- Gold unlock target: peak $250.
- Unlocked coins remain visible and playable.
- Added FLIP ALL once more than one denomination is unlocked.
- Coin Jackpot is shared by all denominations.
- Larger coins add more money to the shared jackpot and increase jackpot chance faster after a loss.
- Coin Flipper automates Copper after Silver becomes available.
- Prestige node BIG COIN is repurposed to SILVER START.
- Scratch / Slot content and balance are intentionally not redesigned in this iteration.

## Questions for the playtest

1. Does having multiple coins remove the repetitive single-button feeling?
2. Do Copper / Silver / Gold feel meaningfully different without needing explanation?
3. Is Silver unlocked too early, too late, or at the right moment?
4. Is Gold something you actively look forward to?
5. Is FLIP ALL satisfying or does it feel like an obviously dominant button?
6. When low on cash, do you naturally return to Copper?
7. Does the shared jackpot make the three coins feel connected?
8. Should multiple physical copies of the same denomination be a future upgrade?

Do not rebalance from intuition alone. Record the playtest result before tuning the thresholds or odds.
